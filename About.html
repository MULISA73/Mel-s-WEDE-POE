About Mel's Cakes & Treats

Mel's Cakes & Treats is a small, locally owned baking business founded by Mel, an entrepreneur with a passion for baking, creativity and creating memorable experiences through delicious treats. The business started from humble beginnings with a simple goal: to make high-quality, beautifully designed cakes and desserts that bring happiness to customers during their special moments.

The journey began by baking for family and friends, experimenting with different recipes, improving decorating skills and receiving positive feedback. With determination, creativity and a commitment to quality, Mel transformed a personal passion into a growing business that provides customised cakes, cupcakes, cookies, dessert boxes and celebration packages.

Starting with limited resources and basic equipment, the business was built through hard work, dedication and a strong focus on customer satisfaction. Every product is carefully prepared to match the customer's preferences, theme and occasion because Mel believes every cake can become part of a lasting memory.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create high-quality, creative and affordable baked products that bring joy to customers and make every celebration memorable. Through personalised designs, quality ingredients and friendly service, Mel's Cakes & Treats aims to meet the unique needs of every customer while continually improving its products and service.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted and recognised bakery brand known for quality, creativity, affordability and exceptional customer service. The business aims to grow from a small start-up into a successful bakery, expand its product range, create employment opportunities and build stronger relationships within the community.
